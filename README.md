# Resampling-Selection-Splines

This repository documents my coursework in Statistical Learning for Data Science as part of the MS in Data Science program at the University of Colorado Boulder. I use R to explore statistical learning methods, evaluate fitted models, and communicate results.

The course focuses on choosing appropriate models, assessing how well they predict new observations, and balancing prediction accuracy with interpretability.


## My perspective

As an Operations Planning Engineer, I am interested in how statistical learning can support operational decisions. Through this coursework, I aim to strengthen my ability to evaluate predictions, recognize uncertainty, and judge whether a model performs well beyond the data used to fit it.

Language and scope

R is the primary programming language for this repository. The repository will grow as I complete course exercises, analyses, and notes. Its focus is learning and applying the methods covered in the syllabus.
